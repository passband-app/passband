About Passband

Passband is an email client for Mac and iOS that triages your Gmail. It syncs your mailbox, sorts it so the inbox holds only what matters, and lets you read, archive, label, and send mail.

01 · HOW IT WORKS

A daemon sorts your mail and the app shows you what matters.

The daemon, squelchd, connects to your mailbox over the Gmail API, syncs your messages and scores each one. Passband is the app you read your mail in.

SourceYour Gmail

Reached over the Gmail API, with the permission you grant.

Daemonsquelchd

Syncs every message, scores it against your rules and an importance model, and files the rest.

AppPassband

On your Mac and iPhone. Shows what made it through.

Triage

Every incoming message is scored and sorted by rules you write in plain language and an importance model. Mail from people you write to always gets through.

Read

Threads render oldest to newest, with inline images and attachments, full-text search across your mailbox, and Quick Look for anything attached.

Act

Archive, label, mark read or unread, and park a thread until a time you pick, when it comes back to the inbox.

Write

Compose, reply, and forward with Markdown formatting, contact autocomplete, and attachments, sent from your own Gmail address.

Extract

Deadlines, verification codes, and package tracking are pulled out of your mail as cards, so you can see the due date or the code without opening the email.

Ask

A built-in assistant searches and summarizes your mailbox, and drafts any action for your confirmation before it touches anything.

02 · YOUR GOOGLE ACCOUNT

Passband needs one Gmail permission.

Passband connects to Gmail through the Gmail API over OAuth, with your consent, using one permission. It also asks for your profile and Google Calendar, and you can decline both.

PermissionWhy Passband needs it
gmail.modify Read your messages so they can be triaged, threaded, searched, and displayed; apply what triage or you decided (archive, label, mark read or unread, bring a parked thread back); and send the mail you write in Passband. It cannot permanently delete mail.
userinfo.profile Optional. Read your Google account's name and profile photo, to greet you by name and show your photo on the account switcher.
calendar.events, calendar.calendarlist.readonly Optional. Read your calendars, and add or change an event only when you do it in Passband. Asked for once at signup; nothing uses it until calendar is available on your account.

The one permission can read and write, so Passband keeps them apart in its own code: the sync path loads its own credential slot and has no code that archives, labels or sends. The Privacy Policy and the trust page say what is stored, where it lives, and who can reach it.

03 · TWO WAYS TO RUN IT

Run it yourself or let us host it.

The daemon and the app are the same either way. What changes is whose disk your mail is on.

Self-host

On your own box

Run the daemon on your own machine, NAS, or server. Your mail and your Google credentials stay on your disk and never reach infrastructure we operate. Open source under the MIT license; the setup is on the self-host page.

Hosted

We run it for you

Each account gets its own isolated instance, which is what lets triage run around the clock and push reach your phone. Your mail and your Google refresh token are stored on our servers; the Privacy Policy covers how. Invitation-only while the product is in preview.
