Passband vs Superhuman · checked October 2026

Faster email, or less of it.

Superhuman Mail is a faster way to do email yourself: keyboard shortcuts, a split inbox, and AI that drafts your replies. It promises to “save 4 hours every week.” Passband is a different idea. It reads every message as it arrives and tells you what needs you and why, so you open fewer emails. Sign-in codes get a sealed page of their own. The rest is squelched.

01 · OPEN LESS MAIL

Know what needs you.

Superhuman makes you faster at the inbox. Passband changes what the inbox asks of you. Every email still arrives. Passband reads each one, pulls forward the few that need you with a line saying why, and files the rest where you can find them when you want to.

For your eyes

Mail with a real obligation or a date, and live conversations with people you write to. It works like a to-do list: an email stays until you mark it done.

Reading

Newsletters, announcements and offers, one card per sender, with articles above promotions. Read them when you feel like it, or clear a whole sender with one key.

Records

Appointments, packages with the carrier's own status, bills with their amount and due date, and today's receipts. Click one and the email it came from opens.

Noise

Everything else. It is filed, not deleted: still in your mail and in search, never on the board and never a notification.

eDone

It leaves the board and stays in Gmail. Done is Passband's own status, so nothing is archived or deleted.

hRemind me

Pick a preset or type a time, like “friday” or “in 3 hours”. Then it comes back to For your eyes.

vFix triage

Move one email in or out of For your eyes, or change what kind of mail it is. The correction is kept.

tA rule in your words

Tell Passband what you want from a sender: only tell me when the amount changes. It applies from then on.

spaceFlow mode

Walk For your eyes one email at a time. e is done and next; s skips and leaves it on the board.

uUnsubscribe

Passband asks first, then usually does it in the background while you move on.

Superhuman Mail

Sections you define.

Split Inbox puts intentional sections at the top of your inbox. You define them with Search criteria like From:, To:, Subject:, or with Auto Labels, Superhuman's AI categories such as Marketing, News and Pitch. A custom label written as an AI prompt needs the Business plan, up to 10 of them.

Auto Summarize adds a 1-line summary underneath the subject line. When a thread lands in the wrong split, you move it.

Passband

A judgment, with its reason.

Triage decides, thread by thread, whether something needs you, what it asks of you and by when, and records why. Your sender rules reach it as instructions in your own words, not patterns to match.

It is a model's judgment, so it will sometimes be wrong. Press v and that email is fixed, and the correction is kept. Press t and you change how that sender is handled from now on.

When the model cannot be reached, new mail waits to be judged rather than being filed on a guess. A correction you make by hand stands: triage does not overrule it.

02 · SIGN-IN CODES

Sign-in codes stay sealed.

Sign-in codes and password reset links do not sit in your inbox between a newsletter and a bill. They go to a page of their own, and they are kept out of search, out of Passband's assistant, and away from any agent you connect.

  • That was me, or not me.Sign-in alerts and resets from the last week ask you to say which.

Passband decides which messages carry a code or a reset link, and a message it has not assessed yet stays closed to agents until it has. A routine “new sign-in to your account” notice with no code in it may still be readable. The full rules are in the threat model.

03 · CUSTODY

Where your mail goes for AI.

Both products send mail to cloud models to do their work, and both say no one trains on it. The difference is where your mail is kept in the meantime, and who holds it.

Superhuman Mail

Stored with a vendor.

Ask AI comes with the Business plan, and turning it on starts indexing 5 years of emails. In Superhuman's words: With Ask AI enabled, Mail stores your emails through a trusted SOC 2-compliant third-party vendor. Your data is encrypted both in transit and at rest.

For Instant Reply and Auto Summarize, we store the queries for 90 days. Ask AI queries and responses are stored to help us improve quality and debug issues. Its subprocessor list names Anthropic, OpenAI, Azure and Baseten as generative AI providers.

And its commitment, stated plainly: Neither Mail nor any vendor trains on your data.

Passband

Your machine, or an instance of your own.

Self-host and Passband runs on your own Mac, NAS or server. Your mail, search index and Google tokens live on your disk; we hold no copy and no key. Triage uses an Anthropic or OpenAI key you supply, under your own account.

Hosted, your account is its own isolated instance with its own database. Your Google token is sealed to a key only your instance holds, and backups are encrypted before they leave the server. Triage reaches Anthropic through our gateway, which keeps no bodies. Training on your mail: never, not by us, not by our model provider.

We do not have a SOC 2 report yet, and the people who run Passband can, in principle, reach a hosted mailbox. The trust page says so, and names every subprocessor and how long each keeps what.

04 · YOUR AGENT

Your agent, briefed.

Both connect to AI agents over MCP, and they make opposite bets. Superhuman's server lets your agent operate the mailbox: 28 documented tools, sending among them, on the Business plan. Passband's Agent MCP hands your agent what triage already worked out: what needs you and why, what is due, what is on its way. It writes mail only as far as you allow: out of the box a draft you send yourself, and sending only if you turn it on. Sign-in codes never reach it. It is available on self-host today and rolling out to hosted.

06 · WHICH ONE

Who should pick which.

Pick Superhuman

If you want to fly through it.

You get a lot of mail and you like handling it yourself, fast. Superhuman is a mature, polished client, and its long-time users praise the speed and the shortcuts.

  • You work in Gmail and Microsoft 365, or on Windows or Android.
  • You work with a team: shared conversations, comments, snippets, a CRM.
  • You want a full calendar in your mail client.
  • You want AI to draft your replies.
Pick Passband

If you would rather open less.

You want to know what needs you without reading everything to find out, and you want your mail in your custody.

  • You use Gmail, on a Mac and an iPhone.
  • Bills, packages, newsletters and sign-in codes bury the mail that matters.
  • You want to run it yourself, or have an instance of your own.
  • You want to hand your agent a briefing, not your mailbox.

Passband is early. It is Gmail only, the iPhone app is on TestFlight, and hosted is an invitation-only preview. Superhuman's costs are real too: independent reviewers name the price, a learning curve one G2 reviewer called “weirdly hard” at first, and drafts that another said “only seems to look at most recent message rather than full context.”
